Taylor's Early Life

Childhood Passion

Taylor Alison Swift was born to an upper middle class family on December 13, 1989 in Pennsylvania. She grew up in a Christmas tree farm. Since childhood, she took up a passion for singing songs from Disney films by heart. She started taking up vocal lessons from the age of 9 and guitar lessons at 12. Although her parents faced through financial struggles, battling cancer, they persevered to show their support in Taylor's passion.

Move to Nashville

At the age of 14, Taylor moved to Nashville in order launch her country music career, inspired by artists like Faith Hill, with her father transferring his job there to support her pursuit of a record deal in the heart of Music City. While she had been visiting for years, the official family relocation happened around age 14, enabling her to sign with Big Machine Records and release her debut album. More recently, she's also increased her time in Nashville to be closer to boyfriend Travis Kelce.

Taylor Publicly Releases Her First Album

In 2006, Taylor realeased her first album, "Taylor Swift" based on her teenage experience. This album contains key tracks like "Tim McGraw," "Teardrops on My Guitar," "Our Song," and "Should've Said No" which are one of the most popular country hits till today.

Switch from Country to Pop

Taylor Swift's official, definitive switch from country to pop happened with her 2014 album 1989, which she called her "first documented, official pop album," though she'd introduced strong pop elements on her 2012 album Red, particularly with hits like "We Are Never Ever Getting Back Together" and "I Knew You Were Trouble"
